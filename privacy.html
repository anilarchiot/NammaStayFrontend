Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how NammaStay (operated by Namma Groups, Chennai, India) handles personal data, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Two kinds of data, two roles

Whose dataExamplesOur role
Customers — property owners and their staffName, email, phone, property details, billing details and GSTIN, sign-in recordsWe are the data fiduciary: we decide how this data is used, as described here.
Guests of properties using NammaStayName, phone, email, nationality, date of birth, ID number and ID photos, passport/visa details for Form C, stay and payment recordsThe property is the data fiduciary; we are its data processor and only use this data to provide NammaStay to that property.
Visitors and enquiries on our websiteName, phone, email, city, message from the early-access formWe are the data fiduciary.

Guests: for questions about your data, please contact the property you stayed at first. We will help the property answer you.

2. Why we use customer and website data

  • To create and run your account, sign you in securely, and provide support.
  • To bill you, issue GST invoices and send reminders about your trial or renewal.
  • To keep NammaStay secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To reply to enquiries from our website.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use guests’ data for our own marketing.

3. Where data is stored and who helps us

  • Data is stored with Supabase in its Mumbai, India region, encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups.
  • Service providers we use only as needed: Supabase (database & hosting of data), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Resend (emails), Razorpay (payment links — in the property’s own account), Channex and booking websites (only when a property connects them), Google Fonts (website fonts).
  • Some providers may process limited data outside India under appropriate safeguards, as permitted by the DPDP Act and Government notifications.

4. How long we keep data

  • Guest ID photos are deleted automatically after the number of days each property sets (by default some time after check-out).
  • Properties can edit or delete guest records at any time.
  • Customer account data is kept while the account is active and deleted or anonymised within 90 days after it is closed, except tax invoices and records we must keep by law.
  • Website enquiries are kept up to 2 years unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

5. Security

Each property can only see its own data (enforced in the database). Staff access follows roles the owner controls; NammaStay admins use two-step sign-in; ID photos are stored privately and shown only to the property’s staff; payment card details are never stored by NammaStay.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to access, correct, update or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Write to thenammastay@gmail.com with the subject “Data request”. We reply within 30 days. Guests should contact the property first (see section 1).
