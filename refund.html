Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy covers payments you make to NammaStay for your subscription. (Payments guests make to your property are between you and your guests.)

1. Free trial

Every new property gets a 15-day free trial. No payment is taken during the trial, so nothing needs to be refunded if you decide not to continue.

2. Cancelling

  • You can cancel any time by not renewing, or by messaging us. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • You can export your data before you leave.

3. Refunds

PlanRefund
MonthlyNot refundable once the month has started. You keep access until the month ends.
YearlyFull refund if you ask within 14 days of payment. After 14 days, no refund; you keep access until the year ends.
Paid twice / wrong amountThe extra amount is refunded in full.
Our serious fault (e.g. NammaStay unavailable for a long time)A fair partial refund or free extension, decided case by case.

Add-ons with third-party costs (for example the two-way channel manager) follow the same rules unless your order says otherwise.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original UPI / bank account within 7 working days of approval, with a GST credit note where needed.

Email thenammastay@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 80985 66636 with your property name and the payment’s UPI transaction ID (UTR).
